Tiny Treads by Tront

Tiny Treads is a free turn-based artillery game in the lineage of Pocket Tanks and Scorched Earth. Set the angle and power, pick one of 400 weapons, fire, and watch the hill change shape. Diggers tunnel, jump jets get you out of a hole, repair fills it back in, and the trajectory preview is there for when you stop guessing. Small tanks. Big grudges.

Play a quick battle against the CPU, hot-seat with a friend on one screen, or online with a public quick join or a private code. Matchmaking runs over public MQTT brokers and the match itself over WebRTC, so there is no account and no server. The garage lets both players pick hulls, paint, flags and names. One HTML file, Canvas 2D, no dependencies, made by Tront (Trent Sterling).
