Tiny Treads

An independent browser artillery game by Tront · Trent Sterling.

Made for ridiculous rivalries

Original code, procedural Canvas artwork, and synthesized sound. Inspired by Pocket Tanks by BlitWise. Not affiliated with or endorsed by BlitWise; no original game assets are included.

The reference-named arsenal contains 400 entries. Classic weapons have individually specified behaviors. Many expansion weapons remain behavior-family interpretations rather than exact reproductions.

Your browser is the engine

Fixed-step ballistics, pooled effects, a CPU material bitmap, and cached dirty-column terrain rendering. Cosmetics never alter a tank's hitbox.

Online, without an account

Public MQTT brokers provide discovery and exchange connection offers. Once connected, match commands and checkpoints travel over a direct encrypted WebRTC data channel.

Players and network providers can observe connection metadata. Names and room presence are sent to discovery services. Do not put personal information in your callsign. Room codes are invitations, not identity verification.

Some networks block peer-to-peer connections. This build does not include a managed TURN relay or dedicated match server. Public discovery is a shared third-party service, not an uptime guarantee.

Garage, preferences, and local results stay in this browser. No analytics, ads, microphone, or camera access.
